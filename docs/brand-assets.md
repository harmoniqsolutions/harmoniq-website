# Manufacturer logo assets

The manufacturer grid uses actual image assets, original colors and proportions.
Existing Biamp, Q-SYS, Crestron, Extron, Dante, Shure and NETGEAR AV PNGs were retained from the repository.
Original marks sit on consistent white logo panels within the dark cards. The existing black/green NETGEAR AV asset is used so every logo stays readable without recoloring.
No recoloring, inversion, recreated wordmarks or partner certification claims are applied.
